About MIIISHOP

Small things, made slowly.

MIIISHOP began with a simple dream: I wanted to sew, even before I knew how. I started with no experience, but my friends gifted me my first sewing machine and I began learning one stitch at a time.

What started as a hobby slowly became a small handmade business. Every item is created with care, patience and a love for practical little pieces that feel personal, useful and full of character.

Good to know

How do I place an order?

To place an order, send us a message on MIIISHOP Facebook page.

Please include these details in your message:

  • Full name
  • Complete address (house or unit, street, barangay, city or municipality, and province)
  • Contact number
  • Product name or design and quantity
  • Mode of Payment (Gcash or Bank Transfer - Maribank, BPI, Metrobank, GoTyme)

Payment must be made in advance to confirm your order.

How will I receive my order?

Orders are delivered via J&T Express. The shipping fee depends on your location and will be added to the product price.

Please note: Cash on delivery (COD) is not available.

How do I wash it?

Hand wash gently with mild detergent, do not bleach. Press between a towel and air dry flat. Don't tumble dry — the quilting will pucker.
